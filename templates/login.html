L2 Review Platform

Every week a support team exports its open cases. This platform turns that spreadsheet into routed review queues for senior (L2) engineers, carries last week's verdicts forward so nobody re-reviews what didn't change, and rolls the verdicts up into a one-page report per case owner for their 1:1.

Pick a role to look around. Nothing you do here is kept, and the data is fictional.
